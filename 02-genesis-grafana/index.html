Why it is built this way

The brief's thesis was "build an agent that uses observability data, and observe the agent you build." So the time-to-threshold prediction is computed from real slopes, not guessed; competing root-cause hypotheses are carried with their contradictions; and every investigation is itself observable. The Grafana annotation at the end is the human's review point, not the agent's victory lap.
